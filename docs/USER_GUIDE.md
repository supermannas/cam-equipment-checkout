# Equipment Checkout System - User Guide

## Overview

The Equipment Checkout System allows Catawba College Communication Arts and Media students and staff to reserve, checkout, and return equipment for class projects and creative work.

## Getting Started

### First Time Users

1. **Register for an Account**
   - Visit the login page and click "Don't have an account? Register"
   - Fill out the registration form with your:
     - Full name
     - Email address (must be valid)
     - Student/Employee ID (optional but recommended)
     - Department (optional)
     - Phone number (optional)
     - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Click "Create Account"

2. **Login**
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click "Sign in" to access the dashboard

### Dashboard Overview

Once logged in, you'll see the main dashboard with:
- **Total Equipment**: All available equipment in the system
- **Active Reservations**: Currently checked out items
- **Pending Approvals**: Reservations waiting for staff approval (students only)
- **Active Users**: Number of registered users

## Making a Reservation

### Step 1: Browse Equipment
1. Click "Equipment" in the left navigation
2. Use filters to find equipment by:
   - Category (Cameras, Audio, Lighting, etc.)
   - Status (Available, Checked Out, etc.)
   - Search by name, SKU, or serial number

### Step 2: Request Reservation
1. Click on an equipment item to view details
2. Click "Reserve This Item"
3. Select your checkout and return dates
4. Enter the purpose of your reservation (required)
5. Review the reservation rules:
   - **Regular reservations**: Maximum 2 nights
   - **Weekend reservations**: Friday to Monday only (3 nights)
6. Click "Submit Reservation"

### Step 3: Sign Digital Waiver
1. After approval, you'll be prompted to sign a digital waiver
2. Review the terms and conditions
3. Sign using your mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen
4. A PDF copy will be generated and saved to your reservation

## Reservation Rules

### Time Limits
- **Regular Reservations**: Maximum 2 nights
- **Weekend Reservations**: Friday to Monday only (3 nights maximum)
- Weekend reservations must start on Friday and end on Monday

### Availability
- Equipment must be available for your entire requested period
- Conflicting reservations will be rejected
- Check the availability calendar before requesting

### Approval Process
- **Students**: Reservations require staff approval
- **Staff/Admin**: Reservations are automatically approved
- You'll receive a notification when your reservation is approved or rejected

## Checking Out Equipment

### In-Person Checkout
1. Bring your student/employee ID to the Communication Arts equipment room
2. Show your reservation confirmation (email or app)
3. Staff will verify your digital waiver signature
4. Inspect the equipment together before leaving
5. Sign the physical checkout log if required

### Equipment Inspection
- Check all equipment before leaving the room
- Report any damage immediately
- Note missing accessories or cables
- Take photos if you find issues

## Returning Equipment

### Return Process
1. Return equipment by the specified date and time
2. Bring all accessories, cables, and cases
3. Staff will inspect the equipment
4. Your reservation will be marked as returned
5. Any damage will be documented

### Late Returns
- Late fees may apply for overdue equipment
- Repeated late returns may result in loss of borrowing privileges
- Contact staff immediately if you need an extension

## Managing Your Reservations

### View Your Reservations
1. Click "Reservations" in the left navigation
2. See all your active, pending, and past reservations
3. Filter by status or date range

### Cancel a Reservation
1. Find your reservation in the list
2. Click "Cancel" (only pending reservations can be cancelled by students)
3. Confirm cancellation
4. Equipment will be released for others to reserve

### Extend a Reservation
1. Contact staff before your return date
2. Request an extension with a valid reason
3. Staff will check availability and approve if possible
4. New waiver may be required

## Tips for Success

### Before You Reserve
- Check the equipment description for requirements
- Verify compatibility with your existing gear
- Read user notes and reviews if available
- Plan for extra time for setup and breakdown

### During Your Checkout
- Handle equipment with care
- Keep all accessories organized
- Take photos of the setup if helpful
- Store equipment in a safe, dry place

### Before Returning
- Clean equipment according to guidelines
- Return all accessories and cables
- Charge batteries if possible
- Pack in original cases when available

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I reserve equipment for more than 2 nights?**
A: Regular reservations are limited to 2 nights. Weekend reservations (Friday-Monday) can be up to 3 nights. Special exceptions require department chair approval.

**Q: What happens if equipment is damaged?**
A: You are responsible for any damage that occurs while equipment is in your possession. Report damage immediately. Costs will be assessed based on repair or replacement value.

**Q: Can I loan equipment to someone else?**
A: No. Equipment may not be loaned to others. The person who made the reservation is responsible for the equipment.

**Q: How far in advance can I reserve equipment?**
A: Reservations can be made up to 2 weeks in advance. Popular equipment may be booked quickly, so plan ahead.

**Q: What if I need to return equipment early?**
A: Return equipment as soon as you're done. This allows others to use it. Contact staff to update your reservation.

**Q: Can I reserve multiple items at once?**
A: Yes. Make separate reservations for each item. Ensure all items are available for your entire checkout period.

## Policy Compliance

Failure to comply with equipment policies may result in:
- Loss of borrowing privileges
- Financial responsibility for damages
- Disciplinary action through the college
- Legal action for significant losses

Always follow proper handling procedures and return equipment on time.
